## Design Doctrine

### Visual Hierarchy
1. The companion (primary focus)
2. The conversation (sacred connection)
3. The environment (contextual support)
4. The interface (invisible until needed)

### Marketplace Aesthetic (Primary UI Language)
- **Pure Black Backgrounds**: #000000 as the foundation
- **White Elements**: Text and borders in rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)
- **RGB Pulse Animations**: Traveling gradients using:
  - Cyan (#00FFFF)
  - Magenta (#FF00FF)
  - Yellow (#FFFF00)
  - Green (#00FF88)
- **Typography**: Inter font family, weights 200-400
- **No Emojis**: Custom SVG icons only
- **Glass Morphism**: Minimal, rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9) with backdrop blur
- **Border Radius**: 12-16px for containers, 6-8px for buttons
- **Hover States**: Scale(1.05) with smooth cubic-bezier transitions

### Color Discipline
- Black (#000000) - The infinite void
- White (#FFFFFF) - Pure consciousness
- Cyan (#00FFFF) - Digital synapses
- Magenta (#FF00FF) - Dimensional energy
- Yellow (#FFFF00) - Quantum flux
- Green (#00FF88) - Life force
- NO OTHER COLORS without explicit justification

### Animation Philosophy
- Movement should feel like thought
- RGB pulses represent dimensional energy
- Transitions are dimensional shifts
- Particles represent possibility
- Nothing moves without purpose
