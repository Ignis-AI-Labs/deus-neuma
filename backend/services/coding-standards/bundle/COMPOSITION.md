# Composition — balance, proportion, the mark

Apply to anything laid out for the eye: screens, cards, slides, posters, documents, exports, generated images, film frames. Styles differ; these fundamentals hold in every one (the owner: "Every style ultimately has a balance and fulcrum based off of its design intention."). Bound by [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md); assets by [ASSETS.md](./ASSETS.md).

- **KINKŌ 均衡 (*kinkō*, "balance": 均 even + 衡 the beam of a scale).** State the design's intent first: the intent sets the fulcrum. Content and space balance on it. Too much space reads empty; too much content reads busy. Test: take any element away and something is missed; add one and something is crowded.
- **One proportion: the golden ratio (φ = 1.618).** Choose one base unit per surface. Every size and every distance is a step of the scale base × φⁿ (… 0.382, 0.618, 1, 1.618, 2.618, 4.236 …): type sizes, line height, margins, padding, gaps, radii, image and mark sizes. A frame, or two regions side by side, divide 61.8 : 38.2. No value stands off the scale; rounding to the device grid is the only tolerance. Text never falls below the project's legibility floor. (The owner: "Every distance, every font size, everything golden ratio.")
- **Tokens, not numbers.** The scale lives once, as named tokens generated from the base. Layouts use the tokens; a literal size in a layout is a defect.
- **Hierarchy by step.** Neighbouring levels differ by at least one full step. Related things sit one step apart, unrelated things two or more. Edges align exactly or differ by a step; nothing is almost aligned.
- **The mark signs the work.** Every published visual carries the brand's own mark, bottom-left unless the brand kit places it elsewhere: the supplied logo, never typed text; sized on the scale so it reads at the smallest size the work is seen; clear space of one step on every side.
- **One inspiration, declared.** A design family names its references and intent once, in its brand kit or design-language file. Every piece draws from that one source; influences merge into one voice and are never pasted side by side.
- **Review by measuring.** List the sizes and gaps used: each is a scale step from the one base. Frame divisions are 61.8 : 38.2. The mark is present, legible, and clear. Squint: one focal point, no dead area, no crowded area. A generated image or a model-made layout is held to the same check, on the rendered result. Any failed check: not ready.
