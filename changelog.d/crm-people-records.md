Added
- Under each person in LEADS, your own record of them: tags (a comma between each), your own fields, and a note that stays on top. SAVE keeps who changed what, and when, on their history.
- YOUR FIELDS, above the people sheet, sets what you want kept on every person: a text, a number, a date, or one of a list. Removing a field keeps what was already written in it.
- A TAGS column, and ALL TAGS to narrow the sheet by one. The people search also finds tags, field values and notes, and the contacts spreadsheet carries tags and the note.
- When the same person is on the list twice, MERGE INTO folds the second lead into the first. Both histories are kept there, and the other address is kept as a former contact.
- FIND ANYTHING, at the top of LEADS, searches people, deals, invoices and companies by any word. A person it finds opens in the sheet below.
- Members read each lead's tags, fields and note in their lead list.
