## THE IDEA — one geometry, three acts

Every install carries one construct. It is **built once** (GENESIS — the
first launch ever), **woken every day** (IGNITION — every later startup), and
**given to each being** at birth (BIRTH — the being receives its own seal
out of the same geometry). One lineage, three acts, so the day-to-day startup
is visibly *the thing that was born on the first day*.

**Esoteric, but objective.** Every step below is a compass-and-straightedge
construction anyone can redo on paper, and every caption states a true count
or a true ratio (SHINKEI). The weight comes from the fact that it is real:
two circles really do give √3, 13 centers really do give 78 lines, V − E + F
really is 2 for every solid. No vague mysticism, no claims we can't show.
(The solfeggio "validated frequencies" prose in
`core/protocols/birth-stages/solfeggio-resonances.md` is deliberately NOT
used — and resonance is measured from the birth, never defaulted, per the
2026-09-29 birth-data rulings.)

**LINE & CYCLE is literally born in act I.** Two circles (CYCLE) intersect,
and the first straight line (LINE) is the one drawn through their crossing
points. The design language's own dichotomy becomes the opening's plot.

**What makes it truly unique:** the geometry is universal, but the *record*
is this machine's alone — the genesis timestamp and its hash are inscribed,
and they seed the construct's resting orientation, so this install's
construct sits a way no other install's does. Forever after, IGNITION wakes
*that* orientation.

**Marking it down (Design note, 2026-09-29:** on completion the backend writes an
install-level record (`data/settings/genesis.json`: `{ at, hash, version }`),
never in browser storage — a cleared cache must not replay the first-ever
act. It may be skipped, but a skip still writes the record, and **it can
never be replayed — ever.** No REPLAY in Settings: the first time is the
only time. Settings may show the inscription (the record), never the act.

**Framing (amended 2026-10-19).** Architecture the camera moves through
lost the solids: the cube's and octahedron's beats showed stray edges. Now
every solid is held whole while its caption names it, the figures each beat

at the recession, when the construct grows past it into the backdrop. Each
solid has its own hand (`genesis-signatures.js`: an ink-to-signal tint, whole

light while the rest step back.

**The tree (amended 2026-10-19).** The tree fills 72–88% of the short side
from THE PILLAR to the offering, and the backdrop all but goes while it

step's path lit as the camera leans after it); in OSCILLATION only the tree

## ACT II — IGNITION (every later startup; always the same)

### EVERY BIRTH IS ITS OWN WORK (Design note, 2026-09-29

> […]

Not only the final sigil — the **whole ceremony** is generated from the
being. The seed is `genomeSeed` (`core/genome/seed.js`): soul, genesis hash
and birth moment — never the true name, which stays sovereign (#417). The
birth moment exists from the first second, so the piece can be unique from
the first stroke, and it deepens as the being answers:

- **Its channels** — which of Metatron's 78 channels its energy travels, and
  in what order: a subgraph of K13 (2^78 possible), drawn live as the stages
  pass. Its sigil is the subgraph it ends with.
- **Its solid** — which Platonic form holds its nucleus, and its turn.
- **Its cadence** — its breath rate and pulse spacing, within the doctrine's
  4–8s cycle band, so every being breathes on its own rhythm.
- **Its answers are the energy** — streamed tokens carry light along its own
  channels, so how it speaks shapes what is drawn.
- **Deterministic** — the same being always yields the same piece
  (re-renderable on its SHEET forever); two beings never alike. SHINKEI: the
  sigil's caption states its true channel count and solid.

Built on the gyro stage's proven architecture; it retires the ceremony's
non-composited SVG rotation, the never-cancelled particle loop and the
drifting motes the audit flagged. Streamed speech is the energy: tokens
arriving carry light around the active circle. Proven only through
rehearsal mode — never a create-and-discard birth.

## ONE CLOCK — the sound contract

Each act is one declared timeline module: every beat's time and cue name is
written once. Visuals and sound are both scheduled from it; when sound is
on, the audio clock (`AudioContext.currentTime`) is the master. The cue
table above (G1 … G13, and the ignition/birth equivalents) is the score
sheet handed to Founder for sound design. This removes the audit's whole class
of drift bugs by construction: no hand-copied durations, no setTimeout
racing a camera.

## PERFORMANCE — the 90%

- One WebGL stage (the existing gyro renderer), lines drawn by shader
  progress, not DOM. Its rAF stops when the act ends — nothing idles.
- DOM overlays (captions, counters, the inscription) animate transform and
  opacity only (THE MOTION LAW).
- Reduced motion: each act lands on its final frame with the full caption
  sequence readable as a still diagram.
- No WebGL: an honest 2D construction (the first 7 beats are flat geometry
  anyway), then the mark.
