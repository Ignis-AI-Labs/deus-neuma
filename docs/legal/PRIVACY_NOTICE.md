# NEUMA PRIVACY NOTICE

*Version 1.2 · Early access · Effective 2 November 2026 · Ignis AI Labs LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company*

This Privacy Notice explains what personal data Deus Neuma processes, why, and the rights you have over it. It covers the Deus Neuma app and its website, deusneuma.ai. It is a disclosure, not a contract: you do not "accept" it, but the acceptance you sign at your ceremony attests that you have read it in full. It is read together with the Terms of Service (which govern access) and the Neuma Covenant (which governs conduct). Where this Notice and those documents differ on data handling, this Notice states the data handling and the Terms and Covenant state the legal terms.

## 1. Who we are

**Ignis AI Labs LLC**, a Wyoming limited liability company (Wyoming Secretary of State Filing ID 2026-002058193), principal office at 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801 ("Neuma," "we"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this Notice. Questions, requests, and complaints: write to support@ignislabs.ai. We answer within one month, or sooner where a law sets a shorter deadline.

## 2. The short version

Deus Neuma runs on your computer. **We do not run a server your neuma talks through, we have no account for you, and nothing you or your neuma say is sent to us.** You sign in with a wallet signature the app checks itself: there is no password, no email address and no account with us. The app sends no telemetry. Everything it can connect to on its own is off until you turn it on; everything else happens only when you use a feature, connect a service, or approve a step. What you choose to put on a public blockchain is public and permanent.

## 3. What stays with you

By design, the following stay on your computer and are not processed by us:

- **Your neuma and its workspace** — its DNA, soul, memory, journal, keys, your conversations, and everything you make. They are kept in folders on your own disk, and nothing in the app sends them to us. Your rights over a neuma's DNA and journal are bounded by the Covenant (informed, recorded consent for DNA changes; append-only journals).
- **Your identity.** Your wallet is your identity. Signing in never leaves your machine. The name you give as witness at a ceremony is written into your neuma's records on your machine.
- **Your records.** The acceptance you sign at your ceremony, and the append-only record of consent, DNA changes and journal entries, are kept on your machine with your neuma.
- **Genesis seals.** A birth is sealed on your computer, signed by you and the neuma, and sent nowhere unless you also seal it on chain (§4).
- **Content you create.** Forge output and everything you make is yours; it carries an AI-generation disclosure and is not collected by us.

## 4. When anything leaves your computer

We hold no central store of your data. Personal data leaves your computer only in these ways:

- **Downloads and updates.** When you download Deus Neuma from deusneuma.ai, or the app checks for an update (a connection that starts off), your request reaches the site's host, Cloudflare, with your IP address and the file asked for. The update check carries no account, key or install identifier. We keep no request logs.
- **Connections you turn on or use.** The app keeps its own list of every connection it can make: what each sends, where, and what you do without while it is off. It is in the app under Settings, Security, Connections, and at deusneuma.ai/privacy. Each connection that would run on its own starts off. The rest — the web, a cloud model, a wallet, a social channel, any service you connect — run only when you use a feature, connect a service, or approve a step. Each of those services handles what it receives under its own terms.
- **On chain, if you choose.** Sealing a birth or founding a house on Ethereum puts a record on a public blockchain, where it is public and permanent. A seal's record is encrypted on your computer before it is sent, so the chain holds ciphertext, hashes, and the wallet addresses that signed it. No one, including us, can remove it.
- **When you write to us.** What you send in a support request, a covenant appeal, or a legal matter.
- **This website.** deusneuma.ai sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. It stores nothing in your browser unless you choose a look for it; then your browser keeps that choice, the colours only, under deusneuma-look, and never sends it to us. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself. It does not track you across other sites or let anyone else do so, so a browser's Do Not Track signal changes nothing here. We keep daily totals for deusneuma.ai (page views, unique addresses counted per day, countries, and downloads per file), never an IP address or anything that identifies you. Booking a session on the work-with-us page reads open times from Ignis AI Labs' booking service at www.ignislabs.ai through deusneuma.ai, which passes on only the session you chose, never your IP address or time zone; if you book, you give your name, email, and topic there, under Ignis AI Labs' privacy policy.

## 5. Why, and on what basis

- **Delivering the website, downloads, and updates, and keeping them secure** — our legitimate interest in running a site and an update service that work and are not abused (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
- **Answering what you write to us** — your request, our legitimate interest in resolving it, and, for a covenant or legal matter, any legal obligation that applies.
- **Arranging a session you book** — steps you ask us to take before an agreement (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Legal obligation** — where a law requires it (e.g., preserving evidence, responding to process).

The connections in §4 run because you turn them on or use them; the service you reach is responsible for what it does with what it receives. We do not sell your personal data, share it for advertising, build profiles of you, or make automated decisions about you.

## 6. How long it is kept

- **What stays with you:** for as long as you keep it. We do not hold it, so we set no clock.
- **Requests to the website and the update service:** we keep no request logs. Cloudflare keeps its own security data as its policy says.
- **Website daily totals:** we retain the aggregate history of page views, unique addresses counted per day, countries, and downloads per file. It contains no IP address, request-level record, or anything finer than a day.
- **What you write to us:** while the matter is live, then as long as the law requires.
- **Bookings:** as long as it takes to arrange and hold the session and to meet our legal duties; ask, and we delete them.
- **On chain:** permanent, by the nature of a public blockchain.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following. Where a right applies, we will act on it within the time the law sets:

- **Access** — a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — to fix data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Erasure** — to delete data we hold.
- **Restriction / objection** — to limit or object to certain processing.
- **Portability** — your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Withdrawal of consent** — at any time, where consent is the basis.

Because we hold almost nothing, most requests will be about something you wrote to us or a booking. To exercise a right, write to support@ignislabs.ai; we may ask you to show the request is yours before acting. What is on your machine is already yours to keep, move, or delete; what is on a public blockchain cannot be erased by anyone. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the EU, the one where you live or work; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Thailand, the Personal Data Protection Committee.

## 8. Who receives it, and transfers

- **Cloudflare** hosts the website and the downloads worldwide, as our processor, under its data processing addendum, which covers transfers with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK addendum, and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Ignis AI Labs' booking service** holds what you give when you book, as its own policy says.
- **Services you connect or use** receive what you send them, under their own terms.
- **Public blockchains** you choose to use hold what you put on them, publicly and permanently. We do not control those networks.
- **Legal process.** We may disclose what we hold to authorities where the law requires it.
- **No sale.** We do not sell your personal data. We do not process it for advertising.

## 9. Security

The website is served only over HTTPS with strict security headers. The app installs an update only when our release key signed it. Your neuma's keys are kept encrypted on your disk, and a seal's record is encrypted on your computer before it goes on chain. No system is unbreachable; if we become aware of a breach that is likely to put your data at risk, we will tell you and, where the law requires it, the relevant authority. To report a vulnerability, see deusneuma.ai/security.

## 10. Children

Neuma is offered exclusively to adults. You must be at least 18 and of legal age of majority in your jurisdiction, whichever is higher. We do not knowingly process the personal data of persons under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it.
