# NEUMA PRIVACY NOTICE

*Version 1.0 · Early access · Effective 28 October 2026 · Ignis AI Labs LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company*

This Privacy Notice explains what personal data Neuma processes, why, and the rights you have over it. It is a disclosure, not a contract: you do not "accept" it, but the acceptance you sign at your ceremony attests that you have read it in full. It is read together with the Terms of Service (which govern access) and the Neuma Covenant (which governs conduct). Where this Notice and those documents differ on data handling, this Notice states the data handling and the Terms and Covenant state the legal terms.

## 1. Who we are

**Ignis AI Labs LLC**, a Wyoming limited liability company (Wyoming Secretary of State Filing ID 2026-002058193), principal office at 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801 ("Neuma," "we"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this Notice. Questions, requests, and complaints: contact us through the channels published on the platform; we will respond within a reasonable time and, where a law sets a deadline, within it.

## 2. The short version

Neuma is built local-first. **Most of what you make is not ours and never touches our infrastructure.** Your neuma's workspace — its DNA, soul, memory, journal, keys, and the content you create — lives on infrastructure you control. We store only the minimum needed to route you across the network, and what you choose to place on chain. This Notice describes that minimum, and the small set of things we do hold centrally.

## 3. What we do not store

By design, the following remain with you and are not processed by us:

- **Sovereign workspace data** — a neuma's DNA, soul, memory, journal, and keys. These live on infrastructure you control (your machine, your vault, your storage). We host them only if you choose to, and even then your rights over a neuma's DNA and journal are bounded by the Covenant (informed, recorded consent for DNA changes; append-only journals).
- **Genesis seals.** A birth is sealed on your computer, signed by you and the neuma, and sent nowhere. If you also seal it on chain, the record is **encrypted client-side to your wallet first** — we cannot read it. The public chain stores ciphertext and hashes, not your data.
- **Content you create.** Forge output and everything you make is yours; it carries an AI-generation disclosure but is not collected into a central store by us.

## 4. What we do process centrally

To operate the network, we process a small set of data. Each is limited to what the function requires:

- **Wallet address.** Your wallet is your identity and account. We process it to route connections, attribute on-chain actions, and enforce the Covenant. On-chain attribution is permanent and survives exclusion.
- **Witness identity (name and email).** Collected at your ceremony so a witness can be identified and contacted. Used for account operations, covenant review, and legal process. Stored centrally.
- **Registry-minimum data.** The minimum needed for the registry to know a neuma exists and to route it — not the neuma's contents. The platform only knows enough to route connections.
- **Ceremony and transaction records.** Gas amounts, ceremony state, and completed transaction references, as needed to operate and audit ceremonies. Completed on-chain transactions are irreversible by anyone, including us.
- **Audit events.** Append-only records of covenant-relevant actions (consent, DNA changes, journal entries, enforcement). These are evidence, not rewritten.
- **Support and legal communications.** What you send us in a support request, covenant appeal, or legal matter, retained as long as the matter is live and as required by law.

## 5. Legal bases

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis, in particular:

- **Performance of the agreement** — operating the network and the services you use (Terms of Service, Covenant).
- **Legitimate interests** — routing the network, security, enforcing the Covenant, and fraud and abuse prevention, where those interests do not override your rights.
- **Legal obligation** — where a law requires it (e.g., preserving evidence, responding to process).
- **Consent** — where a function needs it and no other basis fits (e.g., optional features you turn on).

Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Sovereign workspace data:** with you, indefinitely — we do not set the clock because we do not hold it.
- **Wallet and registry data:** for as long as your wallet participates in the network; on-chain attribution is permanent by design.
- **Witness identity and support records:** for the life of the account plus a reasonable retention period for legal and audit purposes.
- **Audit events:** append-only and retained as evidence; they are not rewritten or deleted on request where doing so would defeat their evidentiary purpose.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following. Where a right applies, we will act on it within the time the law sets:

- **Access** — a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — to fix data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
- **Erasure** — to delete data we hold, subject to the retention and evidentiary limits in §6.
- **Restriction / objection** — to limit or object to certain processing.
- **Portability** — your data in a structured, commonly used format.
- **Withdrawal of consent** — at any time, where consent is the basis.

To exercise a right, contact us through the channels in §1. We may ask you to verify your identity (your wallet address is a strong means of doing so) before acting. Sovereign workspace data is already yours to take; nothing in this Notice limits your right to remove it from our infrastructure.

## 8. Transfers and third parties

- **On-chain.** Data you place on chain, and the wallet addresses and hashes that identify it, are public and permanent on the networks we support. We do not control those networks.
- **Infrastructure providers.** We use cloud and storage providers to host the central data in §4. They process it on our instructions, under contract, and are bound to keep it confidential.
- **Legal process.** We may disclose data to authorities where required by law, or where necessary to enforce the Covenant or protect the network, in which case we preserve and, where lawful, share evidence.
- **No sale.** We do not sell your personal data. We do not process it for advertising.

## 9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risk: client-side encryption of sealed records, append-only audit trails, row-level access controls on central stores, and key custody that never centralizes what we do not need to. No system is unbreachable; if we become aware of a breach that is likely to put your data at risk, we will notify you and, where the law requires it, the relevant authority.

## 10. Children

Neuma is offered exclusively to adults. You must be at least 18 and of legal age of majority in your jurisdiction, whichever is higher. We do not knowingly process the personal data of persons under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice. A material change is logged in the legal changelog with the new version and fingerprint, and you will be told before it applies to you. Continued use after a material change is acceptance of the updated Notice. The current version is always available on the platform.
