## How you sign

On your first pull request, a bot posts a link to this agreement. You sign by
replying with this exact comment:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Your GitHub account, the time and the pull request are recorded as your
signature. You sign once, and it covers all your Contributions.
