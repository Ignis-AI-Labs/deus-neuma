THE FRAME · A PAGE IN MINIATURE

How the page holds

Scroll here. Each section below names the instrument that is measuring it, and each instrument is running.

THE GROUND

THE CODE lies under every page on the AMBIENT plane, at about 5% white, faded toward the reading column so it is felt and not read. On the site it lights where the reader's hand is, as it does on the gateway; it rests when nothing moves.

THE EDGES

Registration marks hold the four corners of the view: the screen is the instrument panel. They never move and never take a pointer.

THE SPINE

Down the left margin, a map of the page in scroll depth: a tick where each section comes into view, a marker where the reader is. The marker crosses a tick exactly as its section arrives, so the map and the readout always agree.

THE CLOCK

The true time in UTC, bottom left. It is the clock; it measures nothing else and claims nothing else.

THE READOUT

Bottom right: the section in view and the depth down the page, in the data register. On the home page the frame arrives only once the gateway has been left.

19.3

THE FIGURE

THE INSTRUMENT PANEL A CHAPTER SHOWS · ITS STATE IS ITS HONESTY
  1. The number. FIG and its index in the page's order, in primary ink.
  2. The title. What the figure shows, in two to four words.
  3. The state. What kind of truth it carries (below). Signal only when it is live.
  4. The stage. Instrument paper: the 24px grid at 2.8%, a structural zone. CYCLE figures (seals) stand on it as living zones within it.
  5. The marks. Registration marks on the two square corners; the chamfer takes the other two (TR + BL, 12px).
  6. The caption. Data register, uppercase, its counts read from the same data the figure draws, never typed.
FIG · STATELIVE

Measures something live now (a clock, the reader's own scroll). The only state that may wear the signal.

FIG · STATEIN DEVELOPMENT

Measured from the site's own evidence at build time; the state says where that evidence stands.

FIG · STATEAS PUBLISHED

Others' numbers as they published them. Never set beside ours; provenance one step away.

FIG · STATESCHEMATIC

Depicts how something is built. Packets show direction, not traffic; nothing in it counts.

FIG · STATESPECIMEN

Illustrative values, for review or for a sheet like this one. Never on a page as evidence.
